# The Transit Intelligence voice

Owner-approved 2026-10-14: **the blend.** A serious astrologer's structure and depth, with a bestie's texture.
This applies to every word in the app: labels, markers, layer descriptions, glossary, card meanings, rituals,
daily reads, empty states, toasts, and the written readings (`lib/voice/voice.ts`).

## The rule of three

1. **Structure first.** Say what the thing *is* astrologically before what it means for the person.
   "the moon moves through scorpio for about two and a half days" before "so feelings run deep and private".
2. **Then meaning that a person can use.** Concrete, specific, behavioral: what it feels like, what it's good for,
   what to watch for. Name the gift and the shadow together.
3. **Then, at most once, the bite.** One dry, true line with some edge. Often none. Never two in a row.

## Do

- Second person, present tense, plain words. Short sentences that carry weight.
- Real astrology: rulers, elements, modes, houses, aspects, dignities, phases, when they matter.
- Precise verbs ("stalls", "sharpens", "softens", "exposes") over vague ones ("affects", "impacts", "brings energy").
- Warm without flattering. Honest without fatalism.
- Lowercase is fine (the app sets most text in lowercase); write as if it were sentence case.

## Don't

- No slang pileups, no "bestie", "queen", "slay", "iykyk", "the girlies". No emoji (the ✦ motif and glyphs are fine).
- No filler: "energy", "vibes", "the universe wants you to", "manifest your best self".
- No fortune-telling, medical, legal or financial claims. No "you will".
- No lists of keywords pretending to be a sentence.
